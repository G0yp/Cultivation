# Gameplay Loop

Cultivate -> Get attribute increase bonus from cultivation method -> farm stats/do quests/fight -> Repeat

## Cultiving

- This is a small minigame expected take about 5 minutes for one run
- There are a lot of small rooms with waves of enemies to fight
- Each enemy killed drop Qi that go towards cultivation

## Attributes

- The 6 attributes
- Strength: Physical damage - increases by dealing damage and doing activities that require strength
- Defense: Physical damage reduction - increases by surviving fights, taking small ammounts of damage, doing some manual labor
- Agility: Attack priority/speed - increased by running/dashing/doing activities that require speed
- Inteligence: The strength of Qi techniques - increases by using strong Qi techniques
- Charisma: Maybe cultivation speed and reputation impact - increases with realm/sub realm
- Wisdom: increases - increases by using Qi

Doing things like farming and blacksmithing can increase all 3 physical stats with different
