# Lore Bible
Xianxia is a Chinese Fantasy genre heavily influenced by Taoism, Buddhism, and Chinese Mythology.


## Cultivation
Cultivation is the practice of absorbing environmental energy (typically referred to as Qi) to strengthen their bodies, extend lifespan, and ultimately achieve immortality.

Cultivation is not a passive process, and characters must actively meditate, consume rare herbs, and practice specific breathing techniques to force their bodies to cultivate.

The Heavens - the source of Qi and the end goal for every Cultivator to reach. The Heavens is the true peak that actively tries to keep cultivators from reaching it by stopping breakthroughs between major realms with tribulations.

Tribulations are challenges that cultivators go through when trying to ascend from one major realm to another. It can be expressed by heart demons trying to take over the cultivators foundation or being struck by lightning to temper the cultivators foundation. 

The five basic elements of Cultivation are the following:
Qi: Spiritual energy that exists in the entire universe. Cultivators draw this energy into themselves to cultivate their bodies.
Meridians: An internal network of pathways through which Qi flows through the body (think blood vessels). Early realms are focused on purifying these pathways.
Dantian: A sort of "core" for cultivators, usually located just below the belly button. A cultivator draws in Qi and stores it in their Dantian for later use.
Cultivation Manuals/Methods: The specific techniques a person uses to circulate Qi.
Realms: Realms are the different stages of cultivation, the later realms allow one to achieve immortality.


## Society
A Xianxia society is usually without specific countries, but rather regions that various groups of cultivators (called Sects) control. Sects act as a school for teaching their specific technique of cultivation, a religious community, and a group of soldiers to complete missions.

Hierarchy in Xianxia stories is extremely strict. If a weak person disrespects someone more powerful than them, it is entirely socially acceptable for the stronger person to kill them. 

Sects usually follow this hierarchy:
Outer Disciple: Mostly used for menial labor and taught very little of actual cultivation unless they have the talent to reach higher realms.
Inner Disciple: Receive payment without working and are taught actual cultivation by mentors.
Core Disciple: Prodigies within the sect, given the finest weapons, pills, and living situations to be prepared for future leadership within the sect.
Elders: Older cultivators who achieve higher realms and are in charge of either teaching or protecting the sect.
Sect Master: The highest ranking cultivator in the sect, usually the most physically powerful, and the leader in charge of diplomacy.

Sects keep a grand library of all the techniques and cultivation manuals they have gathered over thousands of years. Access to the knowledge is gated behind the person's level in a sect. A lower ranking person (like an Outer Disciple) will only have access to basic techniques and manuals, but the higher level a person is in the hierarchy the more they have access too. 

Sects send their disciples on missions into the surrounding world to gather materials, apprehend criminals, and enforce order. Sects reward disciples with contribution points which can be used to purchase techniques or cultivation manuals from the library or purchase cultivation aids like Qi refining pills or Qi gathering formations.


## Orthodox vs Demonic
There are two "factions" found in typical Xianxia stories, the Orthodox Faction, and the Demonic Faction. The Orthodox faction is the traditional "neutral" path of achieving immortality. They value honor and rules, but are typically hypocritical. The Demonic Faction, or simply just the Demonic Sect, follow the path of achieving immortality through forbidden means: stealing souls, blood magic, sacrificing mortals, or simply fighting endlessly. 


## Rogue Cultivators
Rogue Cultivators are cultivators who do not belong to a Sect. They don't have access to all of the treasures, techniques, pills, or teachings that being in a Sect provides, in exchange for absolute freedom. Many Rogue Cultivators are low-talent cultivators who were turned away from sects, their sect was destroyed, or they're a fugitive. Since they don't have any of the aid from Sects, their growth is slow unless they are truly talented. They must be as cunning and exploitative as possible in order to survive.


## Spirit and Demonic Beasts
Spirit Beasts or Demonic Beasts are animals that have passively or actively absorbed Qi over hundreds of years. 

Spirit Beasts cultivate using pure, environmental Qi. They are generally territorial but calm unless provoked, and can form bonds with some cultivators.

Demonic Beasts cultivate using corrupted Qi, blood, or any other malevolent energy. They are typically hyper-aggressive and driven by an insatiable hunger to consume other living beings.

The most important concept regarding beasts is the Beast Core (not to be confused with a Beast "core" created by human cultivators (really a Dantian)), which is a crystalline organ which acts as the Dantian for these beasts. They are usually removed from a Beast's body after killing it and used to create high-tier pills or directly absorb the energy stored in the core. Beast Cores contain extreme amounts of energy, and sometimes it can lead to the cultivator's death if trying to absorb more energy than they can handle.

Some Beasts instinctively nest near rare treasures, as they usually cause more Qi to flow through an area. If a cultivator wants to acquire a rare material, they usually have to defeat the beast guarding it first. 

Beast Tides are a catastrophic event where hundreds of beasts panic due to some corrupted aura, which forms a massive stampede that destroys towns and threatens sects.


## Inheritances and Treasures
When incredibly powerful cultivators know they are going to die, or are about to ascend to a higher realm, they rarely want their lifetime of knowledge to vanish. Instead, they create massive, hidden underground tombs or pocket dimensions to store their life's work and pass it on to a worthy successor. 

These Secret Realms are never handed out, and usually come buried behind incredibly dangerous traps and trials. If a cultivator is able to pass all of those trials, they are granted an Inheritance from that Secret Realm.

In Cultivation Game (name WIP), the main character will stumble across an inheritance that is unguarded, which allows them to embark on their cultivation journey.

A treasure is any item that contains a massive amount of Qi or provides a direct boost to a cultivator's power. Spirit Herbs and Spirit Stones are naturally occurring treasures. Herbs are used to amplify cultivation or increase physical strength for a short time. Spirit Stones are typically used as a higher-cost currency, as cultivators can absorb the energy out of them to cultivate faster.

Treasures can also be manmade, such as flying swords, defensive treasures, or space rings, which create a pocket dimension that acts as an inventory.


## Dao
Dao is the comprehension of certain elements of the world. Examples of different daos in this game would Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Metal, Qi, Karma 
Better understanding of these daos (like higher levels) results in the same actions having a higher impact in the world.

- Fire, Water, Earth, Air all impact the strength and improvement speed of techniques
- Qi increases the speed of Cultivation
- Karma increases the weight of the players actions on their reputation score


## Qi Techniques
Techniques are special skills that take advantage of Qi to do certain actions. This can be imagined as fireballs, teleports, various movement skills, mental attacks, and increasing the effectiveness of weapons. Usually Sects provide both techniques and cultivation manuals to disciples who earn enough contribution points.


## Alchemy 
Alchemy is the practice of refining Qi or Spiritual Herbs into consumable pills. 

Alchemists boil down rare herbs, plants, and other materials using a cauldron they control with special flames to burn away impurities. If the alchemist loses focus, the energy from the ingredients clash, and the cauldron explodes, destroying the ingredients.

Pills are not perfectly pure, and consuming too many can be toxic or create more impurities. These impurities can be directly harmful to the cultivator's body, and can impede their future cultivation.


## Grades
Grades define the strength of Qi Techniques or Cultivation Techniques

Each grade has 9 tiers of proficiency that can be worked through.
Qi Techniques and Cultivation Techniques cannot reach a grade higher than they already are without a special breakthrough from the Heavens. 
When starting to learn an Immortal technique the proficiency will start at Mortal and can level up to the peak of Immortal.
