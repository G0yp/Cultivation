# Realms - 5 Major - 3 Minor
- Qi Refining (Lower, Middle, Upper)
- Foundation Establishment (Lower, Middle, Upper)
- Core Formation (Lower, Middle, Upper, Peak)
- Nascent Soul (Lower, Middle, Upper, Peak)
- True Immortal (Lower, Middle, Upper, Peak)

# Body cultivation
- has 9(can be changed) tiers instead of different realms.
- First 2 can be reached by regular mortals, but after that requires talent
- Body cultivation is a multiplier to stats making it really strong early game and extreamly beneficial late game. 

# Cultivation Manuals
- Has 9 tiers
- Reaching tier 9 is supposed to be hard, but the technique will upgrade through Mortal, Earth, Immortal, and Heaven grades.
- Cultivation is a static increase to stats. The Upper tier of Qi Refining will be around 3 times as strong as a standard mortal.

# Dao - 5 Elements
> Dao's level up when used through the same Mortal, Earth, Immortal, and Heaven grades. Each grade has 9 sub tiers.
- Fire
- Earth
- Water
- Metal
- Air
- Qi - Increases the speed of cultivation
- Karma - Increases the gain or loss of reputation when doing quests

# Foundations
- Need a way to quantify quality of foundation (Mortal, Earth, Immortal, Heaven grades?)
- Basically pills, herbs, and practice will help improve a foundation which will increase cultivation speed in later realms
- Potential for Qi deviation if the foundation is really bad - basically will require reincarnation to reset the foundation and continue to cultivate (Body cultivation will still work), but skills can still be trained
- Poor foundations can be overcome with special treasures

# Body vs Qi cultivation
- Body cultivation: Focuses on Strength, Defense, and Agiliy stats - this can be a multiplier to physical stats and is meant to be strong early game
- Qi cultivation: Focuses on Inteligence, Charisma, and Wisdom - Late game these can compensate for lower physical stats and is meant to out scale body cultivation 

# Stats/Skills/Proficiencies/Daos/Realms - All of these stats are either increased by cultivation (a more idle/passive activity) or by doing activley doing activities to increase specific stats (no point spending for stats)
Stats - Strength, Defense, Agility, Intelligence, Charisma, Wisdom
- Strength: Physical damage multiplier (swords, spears, etc..)
- Defense: Physical damage reduction
- Agility: Attack priority/speed
- Inteligence: The strength of Qi Skills
- Charisma: Maybe cultivation speed (Cultivating expels toxins, which makes cultivation faster and the character hotter) (could affect social interactions)
- Wisdom: Recovery of Qi?
- Hidden stats: late game when maxing out a dao or reaching a high level on a cultivation technique (no idea on what these are yet)
- Talent stat: Makes things easier like cultivating, body tempering, foundation refinement, etc...

Skills - these are related to Qi cultivation and can be attacks or defensive skills (fireballs, body strenghtening, etc...)

Proficiencies - Non combat skills like Blacksmithing, Farming, Tailoring, Enchanting(engraving? Talismens), Exploring

Daos - Each dao will have a comprehension percentage stat that increases through Mortal, Earth, Immortal, and Heaven once reaching 100%

Realms - Realms will also have a percentage for progress and increase by cultivating

Reputation - dynamic system that affects interactions with NPCs and can determine if the character is a demonic cultivator or not. Influenced by Charisma stat
Titles - gained based on actions with NPCs and story events - impacts non combat elements of game

- This is just the explaination for when you die. Nothing (maybe items?) is lost and you respond at your current residence in a sect or city.
